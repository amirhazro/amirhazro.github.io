QuietSwitch Privacy Policy

Effective [release date] · Publisher: amirhazro (individual developer)

QuietSwitch changes your Android phone’s sound and Do Not Disturb settings by schedule, place, calendar or prayer times, and switches them back. Everything it knows about you stays on your phone.

No accounts, no ads, no tracking

QuietSwitch has no account, no advertising, no analytics and no crash-reporting service, and we run no server. Your routines, places, calendar information and activity history are never sent to us.

Location

If you create a place routine, QuietSwitch asks for location access, including access “all the time”, so it can turn quiet on when you arrive and back off when you leave, even while the app is closed. We explain this on screen before Android asks. The app does not record a continuous GPS trail: it saves only the places you choose, and registers them as geofences with Android’s Google Play services, which tell the phone when you enter or leave them. Location is used only for these routines.

For prayer-time routines, the app can take one approximate location reading while you are using it to suggest your city, or you can choose the city yourself. It does not track you for prayer times.

Calendar

If you turn on calendar quiet, QuietSwitch reads the calendars you select to find busy events. Event titles are checked on the phone against your keywords and are not stored; the app keeps only the event times it needs.

Bluetooth and charging

If you use a Bluetooth condition, QuietSwitch checks whether a paired device you chose is connected. It stores only an anonymised identifier and the name you gave it, on your phone. It does not scan for devices.

What stays on your phone, and how to delete it

  • Your routines, places and settings.
  • Activity history: when routines started and ended and why. It is kept for a limited time and pruned automatically.
  • If suggestions are on, when you changed sound by hand (date, time and mode) over the last 21 days, so the app can suggest a routine. You can turn this off and clear it.

Settings → “Delete all local data” removes routines, places, activity and preferences, removes geofence registrations and releases any quiet the app is holding. Uninstalling the app also removes its data. QuietSwitch turns off Android cloud backup for its data.

Sharing, only when you choose

You can share a routine as a link. The link contains only the routine’s timing and sound settings. It never includes your places, coordinates or city. You can also export a compatibility report for support; it is generated on your phone and leaves it only if you share it.

Ratings

QuietSwitch may show Google Play’s in-app review card. Google Play handles the card and any review; the app sends no personal data with it.

Permissions

  • Do Not Disturb access, audio and vibration: to change and restore sound settings.
  • Location, including background location: for place routines only.
  • Calendar: for calendar quiet only.
  • Nearby devices (Bluetooth connect): for Bluetooth conditions only.
  • Notifications: for status, and the optional weekly recap.
  • Run at startup: to restore your routines after the phone restarts.

Permissions that Android asks you about are requested only when you use the feature that needs them, and you can refuse or revoke them in Android settings.

Children

QuietSwitch is not directed at children under 13.

Changes

If the app’s data practices change, this page is updated at the same address before the new version is released, and the date above changes.
