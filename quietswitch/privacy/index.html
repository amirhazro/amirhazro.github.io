QuietSwitch changes your Android phone’s sound and Do Not Disturb settings by schedule, place or prayer times, and switches them back afterwards. This policy explains what it uses to do that and what stays under your control.

Overview

QuietSwitch has no account, no advertising, no analytics and no crash-reporting service, and we run no server. Your routines, places and activity history are never sent to us.

Every feature that needs extra access, such as location, is optional and off until you set it up.

Location

If you create a place routine, QuietSwitch asks for location access, including access “all the time”, so it can make your phone quiet when you arrive and restore it when you leave, even while the app is closed. QuietSwitch explains this on screen before Android asks.

The app does not record a continuous GPS trail. It saves only the places you choose and registers them as geofences with Android’s Google Play services, which tell the phone when you enter or leave them. Location is used only for these routines.

Prayer times

For prayer-time routines, QuietSwitch can take one approximate location reading while you are using the app to suggest your city, or you can pick the city yourself. It does not track you for prayer times.

Storage and deletion

  • Your routines, places and settings.
  • Activity history: when routines started and ended and why, kept for a limited time and pruned automatically.
  • If suggestions are on: when you changed sound by hand (date, time and mode) over the last 21 days.

Settings → Delete all local data removes routines, places, activity and preferences, removes geofence registrations and releases any quiet the app is holding. Uninstalling the app also removes its data. QuietSwitch turns off Android cloud backup for its data.

Sharing you choose

You can share a routine as a link. The link contains only the routine’s timing and sound settings, never your places, coordinates or city. You can also export a compatibility report for support; it is created on your phone and leaves it only if you share it.

QuietSwitch may show Google Play’s in-app review card. Google Play handles the card and any review; the app sends no personal data with it.

Purchases

QuietSwitch Pro is an optional one-time purchase made through Google Play. Google Play takes the payment; QuietSwitch never sees your card or payment details, and we receive no list of who bought it.

To unlock Pro, the app asks Google Play on your phone whether your Google account owns it and keeps Google Play’s signed purchase record on the phone, so Pro keeps working offline. That record is not sent to us. Google’s handling of the payment is covered by Google’s own privacy policy.

Permissions

  • Do Not Disturb access, audio and vibration: to change and restore sound settings.
  • Location, including background location: for place routines only.
  • Notifications: for status and the optional weekly recap.
  • Run at startup: to restore your routines after the phone restarts.
  • Google Play billing and network access: used by Google Play’s billing library for the optional Pro purchase. QuietSwitch itself makes no network requests and sends none of your data anywhere.

Permissions that Android asks you about are requested only when you use the feature that needs them, and you can refuse or revoke them in Android settings.

Children

QuietSwitch is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect their information.

Changes to this policy

If QuietSwitch’s data practices change, this page is updated at the same address before the new version is released, and the “Last updated” date changes.
