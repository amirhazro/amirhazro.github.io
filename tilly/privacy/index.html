Tilly is an expense tracker for Android. You log what you spend, set budgets and bills, and see reports about your money. This policy explains what Tilly stores and what stays under your control.

Overview

Tilly has no account or sign-in, no advertising, no analytics and no crash-reporting service, and we run no server. The app has no internet access of its own, so your records are never sent to us or to anyone else.

Your data leaves your phone only when you choose to send it, for example in a backup, an export or a support email.

Storage and security

Tilly keeps your records in a private database inside the app. Android’s app sandbox and your phone’s screen lock protect it; the database itself is not separately encrypted by Tilly.

If you turn on app lock (a PIN, with optional fingerprint or face unlock), the PIN check and other app secrets are kept in Android’s secure storage, and Tilly hides its screen from screenshots and the recent-apps view.

Receipt photos are chosen with Android’s photo picker. Tilly does not read your contacts, messages, location or other files.

Backups and exports

Backups and exports go only to the place you choose, such as a folder, Google Drive or another app you share with; that service’s own privacy terms then apply. Tilly can protect a backup with a password you set. An unencrypted export (CSV or PDF) can be read by anyone who has the file.

If you turn on automatic backup, Tilly writes encrypted backups to the folder you pick until you turn it off or remove its access. Before restoring a backup, Tilly keeps one encrypted safety copy on the phone so you can undo the restore.

Report a problem opens your email or share app with a short message you can read and edit before sending. Tilly does not attach your records or photos. If you email us, we use your message and address only to reply.

Purchases

Tilly Pro is an optional one-time purchase made through Google Play. Google Play takes the payment; Tilly never sees your card or payment details, and we receive no list of who bought it.

To unlock Pro, the app asks Google Play on your phone whether your Google account owns it and keeps Google Play’s signed purchase record on the phone, so Pro keeps working offline. That record is not sent to us. Google’s handling of the payment is covered by Google’s own privacy policy.

Optional usage counts

Settings has a “local usage counts” switch, off unless you turn it on. When on, Tilly counts a fixed list of app actions per day, such as “app opened” or “entry logged”, for up to 90 days. The counts contain no amounts, payees, notes, account names or identifiers and are never uploaded. Turning the switch off erases them.

Permissions

  • Notifications: for reminders you turn on.
  • Run at startup: to keep your reminders scheduled after the phone restarts.
  • Fingerprint or face unlock: only if you turn on app lock.
  • Google Play billing: for the optional Pro purchase.

Tilly does not request internet access, location, contacts, SMS or camera permissions. Permissions Android asks you about are requested only when you use the feature that needs them, and you can revoke them in Android settings.

Your control

You can edit or delete any record at any time. Deleted records stay in Recently deleted for 30 days and are then removed; small deletion markers are kept so an older backup cannot bring them back. Uninstalling Tilly removes all of its data from your phone. Copies you exported or backed up elsewhere stay there until you delete them.

Because we never hold your data, we cannot recover it for you. Keep a backup, and its password, somewhere safe.

Children

Tilly is a personal finance app for adults. It is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect their information.

Changes to this policy

If Tilly’s data practices change, this page is updated at the same address before the new version is released, and the “Last updated” date changes.
