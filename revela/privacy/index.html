Overview

Revela is an Android workspace for independent vehicle detailers, published by amirhazro. It helps you prepare quotes, document vehicle condition, track work and generate PDF handover records. No Revela account or sign-in is required.

The current internal testing version (0.1.0) stores job data on your device. Subscription checkout and the RevenueCat connection are disabled in this version. There is no automatic upload of job records, photos or diagnostics to us.

Information stored on your device

  • Business and operator details you enter, contact information and an optional logo.
  • Customer and vehicle labels, service packages, quotes, amounts, findings, scope changes, tasks, care notes, acknowledgements and device-recorded dates.
  • Photos you capture or select, report images and generated PDF documents.
  • Preferences, onboarding state, saved care templates and a local allowance ledger. Opaque consumed-job references may remain after a job is deleted to preserve the free allowance.

These records support the features you use; they are not a server-hosted customer database. You are responsible for having permission to record and share other people's information. Device-recorded acknowledgements do not independently verify customer identity or time.

Photos and permissions

Camera access is used when you choose to take a condition photo. The system picker provides images you select. Permissions can be revoked in Android settings. Revela does not request access to your contacts, microphone, messages or device location.

Original photos may already contain location, device and time metadata. Exporting an original can share that metadata. Report derivatives and normalized logos are processed on-device; review your images and export warnings before sharing.

Exports and sharing

When you choose to share a PDF, original photo, diagnostic attachment or encrypted backup, the file goes to the app, provider or recipient you select. Their privacy practices apply to those copies. PDF and original-photo exports are not password-encrypted by Revela and may contain customer or business details.

Opening a share sheet is not proof of delivery. Deleting a local record cannot remove copies already shared or saved outside Revela. We do not sell your job data or use it for advertising.

Storage and encrypted backups

Records are held in the app's private database and files, protected by Android's app sandbox and your device security. The local job database is not separately encrypted by Revela.

Explicit Revela backups are password-encrypted. Backup passwords are used in memory for encryption or decryption, not saved in the job database or sent to our server. We cannot recover a forgotten password or a lost local dataset. Keep your backup and password somewhere safe before changing phones or uninstalling the app. Ordinary exported PDFs are not encrypted backups.

Temporary share and backup-operation files have best-effort cleanup after 24 hours on startup or relevant operations; cleanup is not a guaranteed deletion timer. Recovery evidence may be retained until recovery succeeds.

Subscriptions and RevenueCat

Current testing build: checkout is unavailable and the RevenueCat SDK is not initialized. Revela does not currently send purchase data to RevenueCat or grant paid access.

We are preparing optional Google Play subscriptions using RevenueCat. Before an enabled version is released, we will update this policy and the store disclosures to reflect its actual data flows. That integration is intended to process anonymous app-user identifiers, purchase tokens or receipts, subscription status and necessary technical request information for purchase validation and access management. It is not intended to receive job records, customer details or photos. Do not treat this description of planned integration as notice that purchases are already available.

Google Play handles any store payment information under Google's privacy policy. RevenueCat describes its practices in its privacy policy. Revela does not receive your payment-card number.

Diagnostics, support and this website

Revela can prepare a local diagnostic preview with app/build/platform details, database schema and a random installation reference. It excludes job/customer/media and purchase fields. You review and choose whether and where to share it; it is not automatically uploaded.

If you email support, we receive your address, message and attachments you send and use them to handle your request. Please do not send backup passwords, payment credentials or unnecessary customer information. We keep support correspondence while needed to respond and handle follow-up or applicable legal obligations; you may request deletion.

This policy is hosted on GitHub Pages. Visiting it makes a normal web request to GitHub, which may process IP addresses and technical request logs under the GitHub privacy statement. This page adds no advertising, analytics scripts or cookies of its own. Website hosting is separate from local app storage.

Retention and your choices

You can edit or delete job records within the app and change permissions in Android settings. Frozen issued-document snapshots retain the details recorded at issue time while the associated record exists. Changing your current business profile does not rewrite those historical documents.

Clearing the app's storage or uninstalling it removes app-local data, subject to Android behavior. This does not delete files exported to another folder, cloud provider or recipient. Delete those copies separately. Deletion is not a promise of forensic secure erasure from flash storage. There is no Revela online account to delete, and we cannot remotely access or erase your local records.

Contact us about information you sent directly to support. Depending on your location, you may have rights to access, correct or delete that information and complain to your local data-protection authority.

Children

Revela is a professional business tool, not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect children's personal information through support. Contact us if you believe such information was provided.

Changes to this policy

We will update this page and its date when our data practices change, including before enabling subscription processing. Material changes may also be explained within the app. This policy describes the current testing version; future functionality is identified separately above.
