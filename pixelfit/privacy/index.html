PixelFit Privacy Policy

Effective [release date] · Publisher: amirhazro (individual developer)

PixelFit resizes, compresses and converts photos on your Android phone. This policy explains what the app handles and what you control.

Your photos stay on your device

PixelFit processes photos on your device. It has no accounts, no separate app analytics and no crash-reporting service. Your photo contents, file names and pasted rules are never sent to us and are not included in ad requests. PixelFit reads only the photos you pick in the Android photo picker or share into the app.

Advertising

PixelFit shows ads through Google AdMob and uses Google’s User Messaging Platform to ask for consent where the law requires it. Ads are requested only when Google’s consent tools permit them. Google’s SDK uses the internet and may collect and share your IP address (which can indicate approximate location), device and account identifiers such as the advertising ID and app set ID, ad and app interactions, and diagnostic information, for advertising, analytics and fraud prevention. Google states this data is encrypted in transit. See Google’s SDK data disclosure and Google’s privacy policy.

Where required, you can change your choice at any time in Settings → Ad privacy choices. You can reset or delete your advertising ID in Android settings. Limited or non-personalised ads still involve some data processing by Google.

Optional ad removal

A one-time Ad-free purchase is handled by Google Play Billing under Google’s privacy policy. PixelFit never receives your card details. The app receives the purchase record so it can verify and restore your Ad-free entitlement, and keeps a signed receipt privately on your device. The receipt never appears in logs or ad requests. We run no purchase server. Buying or restoring needs a connection to Google Play; a verified purchase keeps working offline. Reinstalling restores it through the Google Play account you bought it with.

What stays on your device, and how to delete it

  • Photos PixelFit makes, for as long as you choose in Settings → Keep history (90 days unless you change it; “Don’t keep” keeps nothing after you close the results). Signatures are not kept unless you turn on “Keep signatures in History”.
  • Your settings, saved presets and your Ad-free receipt.
  • Small preview images (cache).
  • Private copies needed to recover imports and camera shots; unused copies are removed after seven days.

Settings → “Clear PixelFit history & cache” removes processing history, temporary exports, previews and unused private copies. It keeps the batch you are working on, camera review shots, settings and saved presets. Uninstalling the app removes all of its private storage. Photos you saved to your gallery or a folder belong to you and stay there.

PixelFit turns off Android cloud backup and device transfer for its private data.

Location in photos

By default PixelFit removes location (GPS) data from the photos it saves. You can change this in Settings.

Permissions

  • Camera: only when you take photos in the app.
  • Notifications: to tell you when a long batch has finished.
  • Storage, on Android 8–9 only: to save into your gallery.
  • Internet and advertising ID: used by Google’s ad and consent SDKs.

Children

PixelFit is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly serve them.

Changes

If the app’s data practices change, this page is updated at the same address before the new version is released, and the date above changes.

Contact
