PixelFit resizes, compresses and converts photos on your Android phone so they fit upload rules. This policy explains what the app handles, who else is involved, and the choices you have.

Your photos

PixelFit processes photos on your device. Your photo contents, file names and the rules you paste are never sent to us and are never included in ad requests. PixelFit reads only the photos you pick in the Android photo picker, take with the in-app camera, or share into the app.

PixelFit has no account system, no app analytics and no crash-reporting service, and we run no server that receives your data.

Advertising

PixelFit shows ads through Google AdMob and uses Google’s User Messaging Platform to ask for your consent where the law requires it. Ads are requested only when Google’s consent tools allow it.

Google’s ad SDK uses the internet and may collect and share your IP address (which can indicate approximate location), device and account identifiers such as the advertising ID and app set ID, ad and app interactions, and diagnostic information. Google uses this for advertising, analytics and fraud prevention and states that it is encrypted in transit. Learn more in Google’s SDK data disclosure and Google’s Privacy Policy.

Your choices

  • Where required, change your consent at any time in Settings → Ad privacy choices.
  • Reset or delete your advertising ID in Android settings.
  • Buy Ad-free to stop ads altogether.

Limited or non-personalised ads still involve some data processing by Google.

Ad-free purchase

The optional one-time Ad-free purchase is handled by Google Play Billing under Google’s privacy policy. PixelFit never sees your card or payment details. The app receives the purchase record so it can verify and restore your Ad-free unlock, and keeps a signed receipt privately on your phone. The receipt never appears in logs or ad requests.

Buying or restoring needs a connection to Google Play; a verified purchase keeps working offline. If you reinstall, it is restored through the Google Play account you bought it with.

Storage and deletion

PixelFit keeps the following in its private storage on your phone:

  • Photos it makes, for as long as you choose in Settings → Keep history (90 days unless you change it; “Don’t keep” keeps nothing after you close the results). Signatures are not kept unless you turn on “Keep signatures in History”.
  • Your settings, saved presets and your Ad-free receipt.
  • Small preview images (cache).
  • Private copies needed to recover imports and camera shots. Unused copies are removed after seven days.

Settings → Clear PixelFit history & cache removes processing history, temporary exports, previews and unused private copies. It keeps the batch you are working on, camera review shots, settings and presets. Uninstalling the app removes all of its private storage. Photos you saved to your gallery or a folder belong to you and stay there.

PixelFit turns off Android cloud backup and device transfer for its private data.

Location in photos

Photos can contain the place where they were taken. By default, PixelFit removes this location (GPS) data from the photos it saves. You can change this in Settings.

Permissions

  • Camera: only when you take photos in the app.
  • Notifications: to tell you when a long batch has finished.
  • Storage (Android 8–9 only): to save into your gallery.
  • Internet and advertising ID: used by Google’s ad, consent and billing services.

You can refuse or revoke permissions in Android settings. Photo tools keep working without ads or purchases.

Children

PixelFit is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect their information.

Changes to this policy

If PixelFit’s data practices change, this page is updated at the same address before the new version is released, and the “Last updated” date changes.
